Settings

Face ID

Open the box with your face instead of typing. Your passphrase keeps working either way — Face ID never replaces it.

Family

Keep your family's IDs, bank accounts, cards and logins here too. Each entry is marked with whose it is, and the box can be filtered by person. It all stays in this one encrypted box, behind your passphrase.

Stay unlocked

How long the box stays open when you switch to another app or leave the phone. Longer is easier, shorter is safer. Face ID makes opening it quick either way.

Reminders on this phone

A notification on the morning something is due, the evening before, and ahead of timed things like exams and journeys, plus Sunday evening for the weekly digest. It only says something is due; what it is stays in the box. Only the times go to your reminder server.

Save a backup

Writes one file holding your whole box, still encrypted with your current passphrase. It is safe to keep in iCloud Drive, on a laptop, or anywhere else — without the passphrase the file is meaningless.

The box is getting full. Safari allows this app about 5 MB, and card photos take the most space. Save a backup now, and remove photos you don't need.

If your phone did not offer to save a file, copy everything below into a note or a text file instead — that text is the backup, and it is already encrypted.

Restore from a file

Open a backup file and put its entries back. You will need the passphrase that was in use when that backup was saved.
